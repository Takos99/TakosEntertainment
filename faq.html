Frequently Asked Questions

How do I subscribe?
1. You'll first need to create a Plex account here: https://watch.plex.tv or if you already have one then great, you're all set!
2. When subscribing to Takos Entertainment make sure the email you use is the same email your Plex account is associated with as that is what will be used to share access.
I accidentally subscribed to Takos Entertainment with the wrong (Non-Plex account) email address, How do I fix/update that?
Update your email in the customer portal HERE or send me an email at Support@TakosEntertainment.com with your Invoice # or Order # and I'll manually provide access to whichever email your plex account is under.
How do I start watching?
Once subscribed and access has been shared to your Plex account email (Usually within 24 hours), You will just login to your Plex account on your Computer,Phone,TV,etc. via the Plex App or through a web browser at app.plex.tv and the media will be in your library.
More Questions? Send me an Email.
I'll try and keep the FAQ updated with anymore questions that could be helpful to get started but if you need anything specific or additional help, send me an email at: Support@TakosEntertainment.com and I'll get back to you as soon as I can.
